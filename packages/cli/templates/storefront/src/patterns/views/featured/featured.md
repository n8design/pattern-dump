title: Featured
---

A listing with its first page large, as a feature card (`components/feature-card`), and the rest as a grid of grid
cards (`components/grid-card`) below. With `sort: date` on the folder's `index.md` the first is the newest: for a
home page or a blog's entry page. Every page needs an `image:`, as in the grid: the view names the grid card in its
schema, and the feature card requires the same fields.
