description: The site title with its year, the sections, and the tagline, in one row of labels.
---

At the top of every page: `site.title` (with `site.year`) links to `site.href`, then the nav's sections,
then `site.tagline`. The storefront fills `site` in from `storefront.site` in the config and `nav` from
the pages folder; the sample data here stands in for both.
