---
title: Marquee
description: A band in the accent that scrolls a page's section headings, or the site's sections on the home page.
---

Built from the page itself: its body's h2 headings, or on the home page the top-level sections, separated
by ✳. Nothing to fill in, and no band when there is nothing to scroll. The first run is read out; its
copies, which make the loop seamless, are hidden from assistive technology. It stands still for a
reader who prefers less motion, and on paper.
