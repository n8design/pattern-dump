title: Head
description: The site's icon and theme colour, in every page's head.
---

What the skin adds to every page's `<head>`: the site's icon and its theme colour. Pattern Dump writes the
title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph and structured data itself; this partial adds only what it
can't know, after those and before the stylesheets.

The workbench previews it as a component, so its preview is empty: everything in it belongs in a head.
