title: Footer
description: The footer navigation, the line at the bottom of every page, and the year.
---

At the bottom of every page: the `footer` navigation from `site.navigations` when the config declares
one (the current page's link marked `aria-current="page"`, its group `is-ancestor`), then `site.footer`,
then `site.year` when the site sets one, all from `storefront.site` in the config. The sample data here
stands in for it.
